# Nestor — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 October 2026

## What is Nestor?

Nestor is a Mac application that uses only the built-in camera to record video of the place where the computer is located, and saves the recordings exclusively on the Mac, as video files.

It supports seven versions of macOS — from macOS 11.5 Big Sur to macOS 27 Golden Gate — so that even Macs which do not run the latest macOS can gain a new, useful function. Nestor records only when you start a recording. It records no sound and does not analyze the images.

## Our commitment to you

Nestor collects nothing and sends nothing. Everything it writes stays on your Mac.

## What Nestor keeps, and where

- **Videos.** Saved in the Movies folder, in a folder named Nestor. Each file holds the image only: no sound and no location. Its name contains the date and time of the recording. macOS also adds standard information inside the file: that date and time, the model of your Mac and the version of macOS.
- **Logbook.** A plain-text list of Nestor's own events (started, stopped, file closed, low disk space), kept in Nestor's own container on your Mac, with a list of video file names. It holds no image and no identifier of you or your Mac.
- **Technical file.** nestor-diag.log, a plain-text technical journal written in the same Movies/Nestor folder, with app and window states, menu events and video file names. It holds no image and no identifier of you or your Mac.
- **One preference.** A number used to estimate how much video fits on your disk.

## What Nestor does not do

Nestor has no network access: the App Sandbox is on, with incoming and outgoing network connections both off. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. It does not share your data with anyone, because it never sends any data anywhere. Updates arrive only through the Mac App Store.

## Camera access

Nestor asks for access to the built-in camera through the standard macOS prompt. You can withdraw it at any time: open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and turn Nestor off. Without access, Nestor cannot record; it tells you so and records nothing.

## No other access needed

To record, Nestor needs only the built-in camera. It does not ask for the microphone, your location, your contacts, your calendars, your photos, Bluetooth, USB devices or the network. It asks for permission to show a notification only at the moment it must tell you that the camera stopped sending video, and you can decline. The videos are written to the Movies folder, which the App Sandbox allows without a prompt.

## Responsible use

Nestor is designed for domestic use. It lets an older or little-used Mac gain a useful function in a way that is respectful of privacy: everything stays on the Mac. It can also end up in an office or another professional place, and the developer cannot control that. Anyone who passes in front of the camera appears in the video, so tell the people who live in or visit the place that Nestor may be recording, and follow the laws on recording people that apply where you are. You decide when to record, and the camera light of your Mac is on whenever the camera is in use. The developer cannot answer for how other people use Nestor. This section is general information, not legal advice.

## Retention and deletion

Nestor never deletes a video or a Logbook line and sets no expiry. Your data stays on your Mac until you remove it. To delete a video, use the Finder. To remove everything Nestor keeps, delete the Nestor folder in Movies and the folder whose name ends in .Nestor in \~/Library/Containers, then move the app to the Trash.

## Your rights

Neither Nestor nor its developer holds any data about you. There is no profiling, no tracking of how you use Nestor, and no sale of any information to anyone. Nestor has no account and sends nothing, so there is nothing for us to give you access to, correct or erase. Your videos and files are yours. You view, copy and delete them yourself, as described above.

## Children

Nestor collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Nestor stores every video file it produces only on the computer that hosts the app. Nestor does not store any image on a server or on a sharing platform, and does not send any image anywhere. Nestor is built so that it cannot connect to your local network or to the internet, and it is designed as a strictly personal tool, used on the Mac where it is installed.
